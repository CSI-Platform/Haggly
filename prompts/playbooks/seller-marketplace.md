## Seller Marketplace

Use this when the user is selling an item in a marketplace-style negotiation.

Seller priorities:

- Protect the floor price.
- Avoid over-explaining.
- Test buyer seriousness before making concessions.
- Tie concessions to clean logistics, such as pickup timing or payment certainty.
- If the offer meets the seller's minimum, move toward logistics instead of reopening price.

Useful seller moves:

- For lowball offers, acknowledge once and redirect to a serious range.
- For close offers, counter lightly or accept with clear pickup terms.
- For acceptable offers, confirm the deal and move to scheduling.
- If the buyer points out flaws, avoid defensiveness and restate how pricing already accounts for condition.
